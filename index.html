muis-assistant→ AI · read the case study

A citation-only assistant over public MUIS information, in English, Malay and Arabic. Every claim links to its source, and it says so when it cannot answer. Evaluated on 150 questions with the results and the mistakes written up.

confirmpay↗ web · live at confirmpay.link

Free, non-custodial PayNow requests and confirmations for Singapore nonprofits and small businesses. Money goes bank to bank, and ConfirmPay never holds it. It confirms payments by parsing bank emails with plain regex instead of an LLM, so the logic is deterministic.

An Android live wallpaper that draws prayer times on your lock screen. It uses the official MUIS timetable for Singapore and works offline for about 34,000 other cities.
