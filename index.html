madebyhafiz

madebyhafiz is the home of the things I build. I'm Hafiz, a Computer Engineering graduate from the National University of Singapore, and I make small, practical tools for Android and the web.

Much of it is built around everyday life in Singapore: PayNow, prayer times and public Islamic information. Every project below says what it does and what it is made with. ls projects/ is the shortest way in.

How I build

  • Keep your data yours. Sentryboard asks for no internet permission at all, and ConfirmPay never holds money or stores a bank login.
  • Cite it or say nothing. The MUIS assistant links every claim to a public source, and says so when it cannot answer.
  • Measure it. That assistant is scored against a 150-question eval set, so changes are tested instead of guessed.
